Research Findings

Community garden projects contribute significantly to food security and sustainability. Key findings include:

  • Increased access to fresh produce reduces malnutrition.
  • Community involvement fosters social cohesion and economic benefits.
  • Gardening education improves sustainable food production knowledge.
  • Urban gardens help mitigate the effects of food deserts in cities.
  • Integrating technology enhances yield and efficiency.

Recommendations

To further improve community food security, the following measures should be considered:

  • Government support for urban farming initiatives.
  • Increased education on sustainable agriculture techniques.
  • Encouraging community-led gardening programs.
  • Utilizing technology such as hydroponics for urban food production.
  • Strengthening policies to support local food networks.
