Food Security Through Garden Projects

About

Many communities in South Africa face ongoing challenges of food insecurity and limited access to clean, reliable water for irrigation. Traditional gardening ways sometimes fail due to poor water supply, lack of agricultural training, climate change effects, and weak system coordination. These issues are especially serious in underserved or rural areas where food prices are high, and water infrastructure is poor.

Findings

While we were researching solutions to help increase food security in South Africa, there were cases where farmers and the community were not able to water their crops due to multiple factors due to water. The key problem was water access. There are multiple locations in South Africa, especially in rural areas, where water can be scarce, with some of the issues being poor infrastructure, lack of maintenance, financial constraints because of ageing and failing water infrastructure, poor water quality, and pollution. (Sinazo Sixolisiwe Tshona, 2024). The goal of the AWG is to assist the average user in reducing their dependency on government-provided water. Waterdal one of the rural areas explored during research, highlight that they have been struggling with inconsistent water supply for at least three years, making farming near impossible. (Mafata, 2023).
